About ZR

A Kissimmee detailer who treats every car like his own.

ZR Mobile Detailing LLC started with a simple idea: people in Central Florida work hard, and they shouldn't have to give up a day off sitting in a car wash line. So we bring the detail shop to you.

Florida is tough on cars. The sun cooks dashboards and fades paint, lovebugs etch clear coat, and beach trips leave sand in every seam. We use professional tools and premium products made for exactly that, and we take our time doing it right. Whether it's your daily driver, the family SUV or your weekend car, you'll get the same care.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do you really come to me?

Yes, that's the whole idea. We detail at your home, office, apartment or condo anywhere in Kissimmee and nearby cities (see service areas). For apartments, condos and offices, please check that your property allows it.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

We arrive self-contained with our own water and power, so all we need is space to work around the car. If you have an outside spigot or outlet nearby, let us know; it can help on big jobs.

How long does a detail take?

About 1.5–2 hours for an Exterior Detail, 2–3 hours for an Interior Detail, 3–4.5 hours for a Full Detail and 5–7 hours for the ZR Gold Package. Larger vehicles and very dirty interiors take longer. Paint correction and ceramic coating can take a full day.

Do I need to be home the whole time?

No. We just need access to the car (and keys for interior work) at the start, and you to look it over with us at the end. Many customers go about their day or work from home while we detail.

How do I book?

Use the online booking above, call or text (951) 907-0723, message us on WhatsApp, or comment "BOOK" on any Instagram post. We confirm your time and price by text.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, card, Zelle, Cash App and Apple Pay. You pay when the job is done and you're happy with it.

What if it rains?

Florida afternoon storms happen. Interior work can usually go ahead under a garage or carport. For exterior work in bad weather, we'll text you to move your appointment at no charge.

Why is my price different from the starting price?

Starting prices are for a vehicle in normal condition. Heavy pet hair, sand, mold, stains or very large vehicles take more time and product. We'll always tell you the final price before we start. No surprises.

What's the difference between paint correction and ceramic coating?

Paint correction removes defects: it machine-polishes away swirls, light scratches and oxidation. Ceramic coating protects: it bonds a hard, glossy layer on top so the paint stays cleaner and shinier for longer. Most coated cars are corrected first. Read our guide.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. We walk around the car with you before we leave. If something isn't right, we fix it on the spot.

Do you offer gift cards or fleet pricing?

Yes to both. Gift a detail for birthdays, holidays or a new car purchase, and ask about volume pricing for work trucks, dealerships, rental and Turo fleets. Just call or text.

Ceramic coating vs. sealant vs. wax: what's worth it?

By ZR Mobile Detailing · Kissimmee, FL · 5 min read

All three do the same basic job: put a protective layer between your paint and the world. They differ in how long they last, how glossy they look and what they cost.

WaxSealantCeramic coating
Made ofNatural (carnauba)Synthetic polymersSilica (SiO₂) that bonds to paint
Lasts in FL sun3–6 weeks3–6 months2–5+ years
LookWarm glowSharp, glassy shineDeep, candy-like gloss
Water beadingGoodVery goodExcellent
Cost$$$$$$ (one-time)

Wax

Looks great, but Florida heat breaks it down quickly. Best for show-car enthusiasts who enjoy waxing often.

Sealant

The sweet spot for most daily drivers. Included in our Full Detail and ZR Gold Package, it gives months of UV protection and easier washing for a small cost.

Ceramic coating

A semi-permanent layer that's harder than clear coat. Dirt, bugs, bird droppings and water spots release easily, and the paint stays glossy for years with proper washing. Because it locks in whatever is underneath, the paint is decontaminated and usually polished first. That's why coatings cost more.

What about paint correction?

Correction is different: it removes defects like swirl marks, light scratches and oxidation by machine-polishing the clear coat. Protection products then protect the corrected finish. Think of correction as fixing and coating as protecting.

Our take: keeping the car 3+ years, or it's new or freshly corrected? A ceramic coating pays off. Otherwise, a sealant every 4–6 months is great value. Ask us for a free quote.
